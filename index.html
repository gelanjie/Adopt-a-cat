About us

About Adopt a Cat

Together, we help cats find loving homes.

What is this website?

Adopt a Cat is a simple adoption platform that connects people who love cats with cats who need a home. You can browse the cats we have available, learn about their personality, and submit an adoption request directly from this page.

Every cat on this page has been carefully looked after and is ready to join a new family.

Why adopt?

Every cat deserves a safe, loving home. When you adopt, you give a cat a second chance at life and a family to grow old with. Adopting is a big commitment, but it is one of the most rewarding things you can do.

What we promise

  • Healthy cats that have been checked and cared for.
  • Honest descriptions of every cat's personality.
  • Support after adoption to help you and your new cat settle in.

After you adopt

Once your request is submitted, our team will review it and contact you to confirm the adoption. We ask that you keep your new cat safe, feed it well, and give it the care it needs for life.

Step-by-step guide

How to Use

A quick guide to adopting your new best friend.

1

Create an account

On the login screen, choose Register, enter a username and a password (at least 4 characters), and create your account. Logging in is required before you can adopt a cat.

2

Browse the cats

Click the Home tab to see all available cats. Use the filters for type, colour, age and gender to narrow down your search and find your perfect match.

3

Choose a cat

Click the Adopt Me button on the cat you like. A form will open with the cat's details.

4

Fill in the adoption form

Fill in your name, phone number, email and address. Tell us why you want to adopt the cat and whether you have other pets. Tick the box to confirm you agree to keep the cat safe and care for it in the future.

5

Confirm your request

Press the Confirm Adoption Request button. Your request is saved to our database and the team will contact you soon.

6

Track your requests

Open the My Requests tab to view every adoption request you have submitted. Once a cat has been adopted, it is removed from the catalog.

Tips

  • Take your time — choose a cat whose personality fits your home.
  • Fill the form honestly so we can match you with the right cat.
  • Prepare your home before your new cat arrives.
